# Ron walk baseline independent critique

64 of 64 frames score below 8 and require editing. These scores assess the frame in its cycle, not standalone illustration quality. Character identity is strong; actual alternating gait is missing or disordered.

Method: native-resolution contact sheets, every frame compared against previous frame, including frame 8 → 1. This is a still-frame temporal audit, not a claim of continuous playback inspection. Scores: 1–3 severe missing gait, 4–5 identifiable motion with broken support/phase ordering, 6–7 plausible with visible defects, 8 coherent production-ready walk, 9–10 exceptional polish.

Each direction must contain contact A, down A, passing A, up A, contact B, down B, passing B, up B. Foot travel should follow projected heading. Staff can be carried but its grip must remain attached; do not introduce staff-tip sliding by treating it as planted.

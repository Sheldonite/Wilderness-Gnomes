# Boss rewards and relic abilities

The Squirrel Chief arrives after 60 seconds of active gameplay. Each defeat starts a fresh 60-second countdown for the next boss in the sequence. Level-ups and chest collection continue during fights. See [Bosses and pacing](bosses-and-pacing.md).

The Squirrel Chief drops an ordinary chest. The Oven, Wonky, King Frankie, King Rumbles, and their Encores drop a purple-and-gold relic chest on nearby dry land while relic upgrades are available. Each relic chest grants one choice from the exclusive abilities or ranks. These rewards are separate from the ordinary chest rolls.

| Relic | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crownfire | 120 damage, 210px fire ring | 240 damage, 240px ring | 360 damage, 270px ring |
| Stormcall | 80 damage to 3 targets | 160 damage to 5 targets | 240 damage to 7 targets |
| Phoenix Heart | Heal 8, pulse for 60 damage | Heal 16, pulse for 120 damage | Heal 24, pulse for 180 damage |

Crownfire casts every 4 seconds when an enemy is in reach. Stormcall casts every 3.5 seconds, chaining between distinct living targets within 420 pixels of the previous point. Phoenix Heart casts every 10 seconds when health is missing or an enemy is nearby. All three powers fire automatically, pause with the run, and reset when you restart.
